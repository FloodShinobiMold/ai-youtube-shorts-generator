## 🚀 The Ultimate Short-Form Content Engine

**AI YouTube Shorts Generator** is a revolutionary web application designed to completely automate your short-form content pipeline. 

We all know that Shorts, Reels, and TikToks drive massive engagement, but manually scrubbing through a 2-hour podcast to find the best 30-second clips is exhausting. This tool changes everything. Simply upload your long-form video or paste a YouTube link, and the AI goes to work.

It transcribes the audio, analyzes the context, and identifies the most engaging, viral-worthy moments. It automatically cuts the clips, reframes them to a 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, and generates animated captions to keep viewers hooked. You can go from a 2-hour podcast to 10 ready-to-publish Shorts in minutes.

> 💡 **Why use this generator?** Because virality shouldn't be a manual grind. Let the AI be your video editor, scriptwriter, and social media manager.

## 🚀 Quick Start

> **Three steps. Under two minutes. No installation required.**

### 1️⃣ Launch the Studio
Click the **Launch Studio** button at the top of this README to open the web app.

### 2️⃣ Upload your Video
Drag and drop an MP4 file or paste a YouTube link. Select the target platform (TikTok, Shorts, Reels).

### 3️⃣ Review and Export
The AI will present 5-10 clips. Review the Virality Score, tweak the captions if needed, and click "Export".

## ⚙️ How It Works

<div align="center">

| Layer | Responsibility | Tech |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 🎤 **Transcriber** | Extracts accurate audio transcripts | `OpenAI Whisper` |
| 🧠 **LLM Analyzer** | Finds hooks and scores virality | `GPT-4o / Llama 3` |
| ✂️ **Video Engine** | Cuts, crops, and renders the video | `FFmpeg` |
| 🎨 **Renderer** | Burns in animated subtitles | `Remotion` |

</div>

The pipeline is heavily optimized for the cloud. Video processing happens on dedicated GPU nodes, ensuring your clips are rendered quickly without melting your local laptop.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is it free to use?</b></summary>

The core app is open-source and free to self-host. If you use our hosted version, you get a few free credits per month. Additional rendering time requires a small subscription to cover GPU server costs.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How long does processing take?</b></summary>

For a 1-hour video, the AI analysis takes about 2-3 minutes. Rendering 10 vertical clips with captions takes another 3-4 minutes.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I upload copyrighted music?</b></summary>

You should only use music you have the rights to. The platform provides a library of royalty-free, trending music tracks that are safe to use on TikTok and YouTube.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it support face tracking?</b></summary>

Yes! The app uses computer vision to detect faces. If there is one speaker, it keeps them centered. If it's an interview, it can split the screen vertically.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include the YouTube link you used and a screenshot of the error message.
</details>
